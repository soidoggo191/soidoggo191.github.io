❤️ What This Really Is

This project is not automated. It is not random uploads.

The creator of SoiDoggo191 personally goes out into the streets of Kabin Buri, finding stray and possibly lost dogs — photographing them and recording their locations.

Every photo is taken with purpose: to give these dogs visibility, and a chance to be seen again.
